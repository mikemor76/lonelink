Lone Link

A VR arcade space shooter. Steer your ship by hand, dodge the fire and take down the flagship.

For Meta Quest

The game

Your ship follows your hand. Move it to dodge the swarm, push it forward and pull it back to dive into the fight or retreat, and let the auto-cannon do the shooting. Fight through 50 waves of enemy fleets, asteroid fields and four giant bosses to reach the final flagship, then keep going in Endless mode.

Features

Arcade and Fitness

In Arcade your ship takes the hits. In Fitness the bullets are aimed at you: dodge with your whole body.

Three difficulties

Easy, Normal and Hard change how special weapons are collected and upgraded.

Special weapons

Homing missiles, laser beam, energy wave, reflector shield and a time-slowing field. Catch the same chip again to upgrade it and trigger a powerful bonus.

Bosses and flagship

Four giant bosses, then the final flagship in three phases.

Power-ups

Weapon levels, shields, rapid fire, extra lives and screen-clearing bombs.

Made for comfort

Stand or sit, no artificial movement. Left-handed option and local high scores for each mode.

Controls

  • Move your hand - fly the ship
  • Push / pull your hand - ship goes forward / back
  • Grip - drop a bomb
  • Trigger - next weapon (Easy)
  • Menu button - pause

Details

  • Platform - Meta Quest (standing or sitting)
  • Players - single player, offline
  • Input - Touch controllers
  • Developer - MT & Sons
