Privacy Policy

Lone Link and Lone Link Demo · Last updated: October 2026

Lone Link is a single-player VR game developed and published by MT & Sons.

Data we collect

None. The game does not collect, transmit or share any personal information, device identifiers, location or usage analytics. It does not use the internet, advertising or third-party services.

Data stored on your device

The game saves only your settings (music and sound volume, selected mode, difficulty and hand) and your local high scores, including the name you type for the score table. This data stays on your headset, is never sent anywhere, and is not accessible to us.

Data sharing

We do not share any data with anyone.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the game from your headset, or clear its data in the headset settings, and all saved settings and scores are removed.

Children

The game does not collect any information from anyone, including children.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, write to mtandsons.games@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address.
